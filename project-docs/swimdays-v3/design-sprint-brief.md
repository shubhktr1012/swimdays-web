# Swimdays Design Research Sprint (Optional, 2 Days)

## Goal

Push the storefront from "customized Shopify" to a stronger editorial identity while preserving Shopify-native performance and editability.

## Sprint Outputs

1. 6-8 benchmark references with notes by section type.
2. Final visual direction board (type, spacing, motion, color hierarchy).
3. Wireframe adjustments for:
- Header/navigation behavior
- Hero composition
- Catalog card rhythm
- Lookbook storytelling flow
- Newsletter + FAQ balance
4. Implementation backlog ranked by impact and effort.

## Execution Plan

Day 1:
- Collect references and extract patterns.
- Decide typography, spacing, and interaction rules.

Day 2:
- Create revised section compositions.
- Apply highest-impact refinements in theme.

## Success Criteria

- Homepage has a clear premium editorial flow.
- Visual consistency across desktop and mobile.
- No major performance regressions introduced.

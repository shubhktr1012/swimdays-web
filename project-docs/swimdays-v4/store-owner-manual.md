# Swimdays v4 Store Owner Manual

## 0. Initial Setup Review

- The store uses the **Atelier** theme.
- The visual aesthetic relies on large, calm rhythm blocks and high-quality
  photography. Do not clutter specific pages with too many apps.

## 1. Updating Homepage Content

- Go to `Online Store > Themes > Customize`.
- The Theme Editor is your primary workspace. You can drag and drop sections
  like the Hero Banner, Featured Collections, and Lookbook images.
- Always Preview on Mobile before hitting Save.

## 2. Managing Products & Metafields

- Go to `Products`. Edit title, description, price, media, and variants.
- Keep size variants consistent (XS to 4XL).
- Scroll to the bottom of the product page to fill **Metafields**:
  - `embroidery detail`
  - `care instructions`
  - `value indicator`

## 3. Managing "Pairs Best With"

Swimdays uses the **Shopify Search & Discovery** app to suggest complementary
items on the Product Pages (e.g., tying a swim dress to a specific cover-up).

- Open `Apps > Search & Discovery`.
- Go to `Recommendations`.
- Select your main product, and under "Complementary products" click "Add
  products".
- Select 2-3 items. This updates the block immediately on the product page.

## 4. Newsletters and Emails

- The footer/homepage newsletter signup form automatically tags customers with
  `newsletter`.
- Use the **Shopify Email** app to design and send campaigns using the email
  list generated natively.

## 5. Basic Maintenance & QA

- Check homepage on mobile and desktop after publishing a new collection.
- Confirm Links in the Footer Menu map correctly to your legal pages (Settings >
  Policies).

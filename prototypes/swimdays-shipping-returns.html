Return and Exchange

At Swimdays, we want the post-purchase experience to feel just as calm and considered as the pieces themselves. Please review the conditions below before requesting an exchange or reporting an issue.

For any assistance, please email studioswimdays@gmail.com.

Swimdays does not offer refunds on swimwear. Bodysuits, shorts, and leggings are not eligible for return or exchange because of hygiene standards. For size- or style-based exchanges, please raise a request within 7 days of delivery and ensure the swimwear is unused, unwashed, and intact with its original packaging. For damaged or incorrect items, please raise a concern within 24 to 48 hours of receiving the product. To help process a claim efficiently, please begin recording before opening the package seal and share a clear unboxing video.

Initiating a Return or Exchange

Please email studioswimdays@gmail.com for any concerns related to damaged or incorrect items. Swimdays offers one-time free shipping for approved exchanges. Once your request is approved, a pickup will be arranged.

  • Unauthorized returns or exchanges will not be accepted.
  • If a pickup location is not serviceable, the customer will be asked to ship the product back.
  • Returns or exchanges for express courier service must be initiated by the customer and the shipping charges must be borne by the customer.

Cancellations

If you need to cancel an order, please write to studioswimdays@gmail.com within 24 to 48 hours of placing the order so the process can be initiated.

Shipping

We know you must be eager to make waves in your Swimdays pieces. We are constantly working to deliver your orders on time.

  • Swimdays currently ships across India and to the UAE.
  • Orders are dispatched within 1 to 2 business days after payment confirmation.
  • Domestic orders typically take an additional 4 to 5 business days for delivery, depending on the PIN code.
  • Domestic shipping is free.
  • Express courier service is available at a flat ₹350.
  • A partial COD payment option is available at checkout.
  • For international orders, delivery charges and estimated timelines are calculated after the order is placed. Customs duties, taxes, and other fees remain the customer’s responsibility.
  • Tracking details are shared once the order is confirmed.

Lost or Stolen Packages

Swimdays is not responsible for lost or stolen packages. Please make sure your shipping address is entered correctly at checkout to avoid delivery issues. Once the package is handed to the shipping carrier, the shipment becomes the carrier’s responsibility.

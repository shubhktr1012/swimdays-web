Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer refunds?

No. Swimdays does not offer refunds on swimwear.

Read full policy
What items are non-returnable?

Bodysuits, shorts, and leggings are not returnable or exchangeable because of hygiene standards.

See eligibility details
How do I request an exchange?

Email Swimdays to raise the concern. Once the request is approved, a one-time exchange pickup can be arranged.

See the request process
How fast do orders ship?

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation and domestic delivery usually takes another 4–5 business days.

Read shipping details
Do you offer express delivery?

Yes. Express courier service is available at a flat ₹350.
